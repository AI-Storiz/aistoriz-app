How to request deletion

If you created an APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER account, you can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data using either method below:

  1. In the app: open Profile → Delete Account, then send a deletion request from your registered email address.
  2. By email: contact us at the address below from the email linked to your account.

SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER

What to include in your request

Please include the email address you used to register for APP_NAME_PLACEHOLDER. If you signed in with a third-party provider, include that email and note the sign-in method so we can verify your account.

Subscriptions (Google Play / App Store)

If you have an active subscription, cancel it in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings before or when you request account deletion. Canceling a subscription does not automatically delete your account.

What we delete

When we process a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize:

  • Your user account and login credentials
  • Saved story prompts and generated comics on our servers
  • Uploaded character photos and related metadata
  • App usage history, credits history, and referral data tied to your account
  • Push notification tokens associated with your account

Deletion is permanent. Disabling or logging out of your account does not qualify as account deletion.

What we may retain

We may retain limited information only where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or payment and tax records. Any retained data is minimized and protected. Details are also described in our Privacy Policy.

Processing time

Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days. We will confirm by email when deletion is complete, when possible.
